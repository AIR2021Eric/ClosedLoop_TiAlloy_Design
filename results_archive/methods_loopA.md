# Methods — Loop A

The search treated composition as an integer atomic-percent vector on the palette Ti–Al–V–Mo–Zr–Sn–Cr–Fe–Nb. A repair step projected every proposal onto that integer lattice with the components summing to 100 at%, inside the caps in `framework/config.py` (`CAPS_AT`). Density is the inverse rule-of-mixtures. A composition passed the inline gates when Mo-equivalent was at most 16 wt%, Al-equivalent was at most 9 wt%, and density lay in 3.8–4.8 g/cm³. The phase-field label follows the Mo-equivalent: near-α below 2, α+β below 10, and metastable-β otherwise.

The two objectives are the weight-free Ashby indices. Strength is σ_y,aged^(2/3) / ρ and stiffness is E_proxy^(1/2) / ρ, both maximized. E_proxy is the isotropic modulus from the rule-of-mixtures bulk and shear moduli. The CHGNet median modulus is stored as E_med and stays out of the Ashby index. Yield strength is the closed-form B_aged scenario: a phase-field base, plus 400 MPa for each unit of solute fraction above 0.10, plus a Hall–Petch term. Fitness uses the reference grain size of 10 µm, so that Hall–Petch increment is zero. `strength.ss_edge_flag` marks `SS_LINEAR_EDGE` when the solute fraction is at least 0.20 away from 0.10. A stored value of 0.30 falls one floating-point step short of that gap, so Ti70Al15V14Cr1 and Ti70Al15V13Cr2 are flagged OK. Ti69Al15V16 (0.31), Ti66Al15V19 (0.34), and Ti67Al15V17Cr1 (0.33) carry the flag. These strengths are the declared scenario. They are not measured tensile values.

Proposals came from NSGA-II (population 200, 40 generations), warm-started from the leading Ashby members of the live archive. After repair and the inline gates, the queue dropped any formula already in the certified or provisional archive. A composition that had been screened and then left out of both archives could be proposed again. A Gaussian process (RBF kernel with a white-noise term, inputs standardized) was fit to the unique formulas that already had a CHGNet C44 median, one label per formula. The descriptors are Mo_eq, Al_eq, c_solute, misfit_delta, and G_rm. The acquisition score is the upper confidence bound μ + 2σ. That training-set size, written to the ledger as `gp_train`, reached 166 at iteration 7 and stayed there through iteration 10.

Each real iteration spent a fixed CHGNet 0.3 budget on a 54-atom special-quasirandom cell with finite-difference C11, C12, and C44. Tier S screened up to 50 formulas with one run (seed 42, strain amplitude 0.015) and advanced a formula when C44_med > 0. Tier F took up to 10 of those, three seeds, and advanced a formula when the Born-violation fraction was at most 0.50. Tier U took four formulas — two reserved for the highest-strength provisionals that still had fewer than nine runs, the other two by the acquisition score — at three seeds and three strain amplitudes (nine runs). A run is a Born violation when C44 ≤ 0 or C11 − C12 ≤ 0.

The archive accepted a formula as certified when it had at least nine runs, a violation fraction below 0.20, a pass of the inline gates, and a pass of the soft gates. The soft gates require Pugh’s ratio K/G ≥ 1.75 and a hull distance ≤ 0.05 eV/atom when that quantity is already known for the formula; a pending hull or Pugh does not by itself reject the row. A formula with at least three runs and a violation fraction below 0.50 stayed provisional. A violation fraction of 0.50 or higher, or a known soft-gate failure, vetoed it. Certifying a formula removed its provisional row. CrabNet, Roost, ALIGNN, and pycalphad are not called inside the loop. Before any iteration was committed, the v1 equivalence gate had to reproduce the locked datasheet mean absolute errors of 1.87, 1.00, and 7.20 on raw density, calibrated density, and yield strength.

The stopping rule was declared before the later iterations and was not revised. It stops the search on two consecutive real iterations that add no certified alloy and gain less than 1% certified-front hypervolume, or when the real-iteration count reaches 10. Hypervolume is the exact two-objective volume of the certified-only non-dominated set against the reference point (21.0, 2.25). The GP-label count and the Tier-S kill rate are printed and do not gate. Iteration 10 reached the cap. The stagnation clause had not fired: the ledger still recorded new certifications, the hypervolume streak stayed at 0, and the certified-front hypervolume was 1.0740 from iteration 8 through iteration 10. The driver parsed the two-space verdict from `stopping_check.py`, wrote the tag `v2.0-loop-closed`, and returned exit code 10.
